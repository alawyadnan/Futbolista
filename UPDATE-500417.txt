Futbolista 500417 — local update, not published

What changed
1. Mobile statistics table: Results, Goals, Awards and All views. Compact views
   show the player and three relevant statistics without horizontal scrolling.
   All keeps every existing column; the selected sorting statistic stays visible.
   Removed the duplicate table shortcut strip, not any sorting capability.
2. Match details: switch between the team sheets and paired statistics for
   participants, normal goals, distinct goalscorers and opponent own goals.
   Scorer contributions link to player profiles. Voting remains unchanged.
3. Player profile: monthly appearances, wins/draws/losses and goals. The latest
   four active months appear first; earlier months remain expandable. Selecting
   a month opens the existing player-specific match history with that filter.
4. Dashboard: redesigned top-scorer rows, clearer goal totals and relative bars,
   existing avatars, correct tied ranks and no earned rank for zero goals.
5. Arabic/English labels, keyboard focus and touch-sized controls. Preserved the
   Safari table scroll fix and corrected wrapping of ranks 10 and above.
